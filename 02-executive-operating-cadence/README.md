# Executive Operating Cadence

### A management system for turning strategy into decisions, accountability, and execution.

Strategy does not execute itself.

Organizations need a disciplined management rhythm that connects priorities, performance, decisions, resources, and accountability.

The purpose of an operating cadence is not to create more meetings.

It is to create a system in which leaders can see what is happening, understand why, make decisions, assign ownership, and act before problems compound.

> **A management meeting should change what happens next.**

---

## The Operating Logic

Every management cycle should connect:

**Strategy → Priorities → KPIs → Performance → Diagnosis → Decisions → Actions → Accountability → Learning**

Leadership should consistently be able to answer:

1. What are our priorities?
2. How are we performing?
3. Where are we off plan?
4. Why?
5. What decision is required?
6. Who owns the response?
7. What happens next?
8. When will we know whether it worked?

If these questions cannot be answered consistently, the operating cadence is not working.

---

## Management Architecture

Different decisions require different management horizons.

| Cadence | Primary Purpose | Horizon | Core Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weekly Operating Review** | Execution and constraint removal | 7 to 30 days | Decisions and actions |
| **Monthly Business Review** | Business and financial performance | 30 to 90 days | Corrective interventions |
| **Quarterly Business Review** | Strategic progress and resource allocation | 3 to 12 months | Strategic adjustments |
| **Annual Strategy Cycle** | Direction, choices, priorities, and investment | 12 to 36 months | Strategic plan |

These are not four versions of the same meeting.

Each exists to make a different class of decision.

## 05. Executive KPI Architecture

Create a disciplined management information system.

Separate metrics into four categories:

**Outcomes**

What results are we producing?

**Drivers**

What creates those outcomes?

**Health Indicators**

Can the organization sustain performance?

**Strategic Indicators**

Are we progressing against the strategy?

Every critical KPI should have:

**Definition → Target → Owner → Frequency → Source → Action Threshold**

# Management Principles

### Manage by exception

Executive attention is scarce.

Spend disproportionate time on material deviations, constraints, risks, and decisions.

### Separate information from discussion

Information should arrive before the meeting whenever possible.

Meeting time should be used for diagnosis, decisions, trade-offs, and action.

### Make ownership explicit

Every material action requires one accountable owner.

Shared participation does not mean shared accountability.

### Connect decisions to outcomes

A decision is incomplete without an expected outcome and a review point.

### Challenge the cadence itself

Recurring meetings are part of the operating model.

They should earn the resources they consume.

Ask periodically:

> **What would stop happening if we cancelled this meeting?**

If the answer is unclear, redesign or remove it.

# Executive Standard

At any point, leadership should know:

**What matters most.**

**How the business is performing.**

**Where performance is deviating.**

**What is causing the deviation.**

**What decision has been made.**

**Who owns the response.**

**What happens next.**

**How success will be measured.**

That is management visibility.

The objective is not control for its own sake.

The objective is an organization capable of executing with clarity and accountability.
